Pacing

Actual vs. Target, by SBU

For each SBU: the most recent day on record, this month's target OEE%, and how the SBU is pacing month-to-date against that target. Positive numbers (green) mean ahead of target; negative (red) means behind.

How the component targets are derived: OEE% = Availability% × Performance% × Quality%. The target sheet only gives one monthly OEE% target per SBU, so the "Expected" values for Availability, Performance, and Quality are each the cube root of that monthly OEE target — multiplying the three expected values back together reproduces the OEE target exactly. It's an even split across the three factors, not a target Ops actually set per-factor, so treat those three deltas as directional.

Monthly Target and Baseline always reflect the real current month (or the "as of" date you pick below) — not whichever month an SBU's most recent actual data happens to fall in. That means for an SBU whose latest recorded data lags behind (e.g. still on Jan/Feb), its "Today (actual)" and "Actual MTD" figures are older data being compared against a more current target — worth keeping in mind when reading the gap.
